EMBER COURT

Duel of the Four Winds

Outplay Sable, the Storm Warden, in a duel of elemental cards. Ember burns Gale, Gale wears down Stone, Stone dams Tide, Tide douses Ember.

How to play

  • Each round, you and Sable each play one card face down, then reveal.
  • Higher power wins the clash. Element advantage adds +3 power.
  • The loser takes damage equal to the power gap (min 1). A tied clash hurts you both.
  • Mend heals 6 · Ward blocks the next hit · Siphon steals 4 life.
  • Win 2 rounds in a row to gain Momentum: your next card gets +2 power.
  • First to 0 life loses. If the decks run out, the higher life total wins.

Mouse: click a card to play it  ·  Keyboard: 1–5 play a card, Enter start, R play again
